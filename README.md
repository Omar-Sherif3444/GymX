# GymX — Smart Gym Experience 💪

**GymX** is a customizable digital platform designed to connect gyms with their members through one smart, user-friendly website.

The platform aims to improve the gym experience by combining membership management, attendance tracking, equipment guides, gamification, and a private fitness community.

## 📌 Project Overview

GymX serves both gym members and gym management. Each gym can customize the platform according to its available equipment, services, and community.

Instead of being just an informational website, GymX aims to provide a complete digital gym experience where members can manage their subscriptions, track their visits, learn about gym equipment, and engage with other members.

## ✨ Key Features

### 👤 Member Accounts

- Personal member accounts.
- View subscription details and expiration dates.
- Check the remaining subscription period.
- Access a personal QR code.

### 📅 Attendance Tracking

- View gym attendance history.
- Track check-in and check-out times.
- Keep attendance records organized.

### 🏋️ Equipment Guides

- Display equipment available at each gym.
- Explain the muscles targeted by each machine.
- Provide instructions for correct equipment usage.
- Explore different techniques and exercises.

### 🎮 Gamification & Rewards

- Earn tokens by completing activities.
- Participate in fitness challenges.
- Introduce mini-games and side quests.
- Reward members for their participation and achievements.

### 🤝 Private Gym Community

- Interact with other gym members.
- Share fitness achievements.
- Participate in community challenges.
- Post fitness-related content.

### ⚙️ Gym Customization

- Customize the platform for each gym.
- Display equipment and services specific to the gym.
- Adapt the experience to the gym's needs and community.

## 🎯 Project Goals

- Improve communication between gyms and their members.
- Make membership and attendance information easier to access.
- Help members use gym equipment correctly.
- Encourage consistency and engagement through challenges and rewards.
- Build a connected fitness community.
- Provide a customizable digital solution for different gyms.

## 👥 Target Users

- **Gym Members:** Manage subscriptions, view attendance records, learn about equipment, and participate in community activities.
- **Gym Management:** Provide members with a centralized digital platform for gym-related information and services.
